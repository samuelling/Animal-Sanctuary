# Animal Sanctuary

A 16-bit style creature sanctuary game, dreamed up by Maya & Sam.

Explore magical wilds, befriend glowing creatures, bring them home to your sanctuary, and keep them happy so visitors pay to see them. Spend coins on new habitats and maps to new zones.

**Companions:** bring a creature along on an expedition and its ability works in the wilds — some lure creatures that only appear for them (a water song, a glow, a fire companion), others make catching easier, find more berries or treasure, or let you cross lava.

**Build mode:** tap Build to lay out your sanctuary Zoo Tycoon style: place habitats (meadow, pond, moon grove, ember rock), draw paths, and decorate with flowers, hedges, trees, ponds, benches, lamps, fountains and snack stands. Visitors walk only on paths and tip at habitats they pass, so every habitat needs a path beside it. Decorations add Charm, which raises tips.

**Willowbrook town:** walk out the bottom entrance of the sanctuary to reach town. Mabel's Market sells berries, golden apples and lucky clovers; Rosa's Bakery sells creature cookies; Bram's Builders sells grand habitats, new decorations, extra land and a cottage extension; Pip's Home Goods sells furniture, wallpaper and floors. Townsfolk with a yellow "!" have quests that unlock new areas and rewards. Trails at the edges of town lead to each wild area.

**Pop and Gran:** your Pop (the sanctuary's first keeper) and Gran live in the little house on the west side of Willowbrook. Visit every day to sit and talk (his stories are saved in your journal), bring berry tea, show him your creatures, or help around the house. Over the seasons Pop's memory slowly fades: he forgets, gets confused, wanders off once, and grows very tired. His story only moves forward in seasons you've visited him. Along the way, moments of confusion let you choose how to respond, and the game gently shows that kindness and going along help more than correcting. After his last season Pop dies peacefully in his sleep. You wake to his letter, the town gathers in his garden to remember him, and a tree is planted that grows a little every season. Then you help Gran through her grief, season by season, until she finds joy again.

**Lifetimes:** creatures grow from young to grown to elder (elders nap more and move slower), and one day a creature passes away peacefully in its habitat. Visit to say goodbye and lay it to rest; it's remembered in the journal's "In loving memory" page, and the habitat is ready for a new friend. A well-loved creature sometimes leaves a little one behind. In the journal's Settings you can switch creature lifetimes off (nobody ages while off), and switch on **Keeper's lifetime**: your keeper grows older over 1, 3 or 6 in-game years, with silver hair, slower steps, a cane, and shakier hands when befriending creatures. At the end of that life comes an epilogue built from your keeper's own story, and the credits roll. Then you can begin again as the next keeper.

**Maple Lane houses:** at the bottom of Willowbrook are four lots for sale. Walk up to a For Sale sign to build a house: a Guest cottage, Bunkhouse, Treehouse, Lakeside cabin, Grand manor or Castle tower (the fancier ones unlock at higher Sanctuary Ranks). Every house comes with a bed and has its own inside to decorate with your furniture, wallpaper and floors. Walk to the nameplate by the door to name it, so when you play together each player can have a house of their own to sleep in.

**Your cottage:** the purple-roofed house in town. Tap Decorate inside to place furniture (use the Turn tool, or press R, to turn it: long pieces can go sideways), change wallpaper and floors, nap in your bed, and play the piano.

**Wardrobe (Emmy's idea):** change how your keeper looks. Open your Journal and tap Wardrobe, or use the wardrobe in your cottage (the Magic mirror works too). Choose girl, boy or neither; a hairstyle (short, long, ponytail, pigtails or buzzed); skin color; an outfit (shirt and pants, a dress or overalls) and its colors; a hat (cap, beanie, bow, witch hat, crown or none); and an extra (sunglasses, freckles or rosy cheeks). Turn around to see every side, or tap Surprise me. Your look is saved on your device, and when you play together your partner sees you as you made yourself.

**Sanctuary Rank (★ to ★★★★★):** everything in the game gets fancier as you grow. Befriend more kinds of creatures, finish quests and welcome visitors to climb from Sprout Keeper to Cozy, Fancy, Legendary and finally Mythic Keeper. Each rank brings new, pricier and more amazing things to every shop: crystal trees, rainbow arches, golden fountains, star towers, a carousel, a golden dragon statue and an aurora beacon for the sanctuary; rainbow cakes, moon cakes and star fruit (a rare creature right away) in town; and for the cottage, sofas, telescopes, an arcade, a jukebox, a chandelier, a throne, a dragon's hoard, an aurora window and a cloud bed. Locked items show exactly what you need to do. Some trophies can never be bought and are only earned by befriending legendary creatures. Check your rank on the ranger board or in your journal.

**The secret portal to Watertown:** once you reach Sanctuary Rank 2, a shimmering portal appears in a clearing in the trees east of Willowbrook's Town Hall. Step through and you arrive in the real Watertown, Massachusetts: Watertown Square, the Charles River and the neighborhoods around Irving Street, built street by street from the map data in [Townies](https://github.com/samuelling/Townies) (OpenStreetMap and MassGIS). Street and park names show as you walk, real shops have signs, and townsfolk stroll the sidewalks. Four creatures live only here: the Acornsprite, the Lampwisp, the Charlotter (a Charles River otter) and the legendary Glimmerheron. Seven townsfolk have quests worth thousands of coins in total. Finish them all to earn the Key to the City and plans for the Watertown Clock Tower, the grandest wonder in the game (+80 Charm). Treasure there is worth triple, and a chest under the bridge refills every day.

The portal drops you in front of the Halloween House on Irving Street, with a skeleton-and-skull lawn lit up red, jack-o'-lanterns, tombstones, a bubbling cauldron and bats circling the roof. Papa Bones, Gemma, Mara, Remy and Rhett hang out in the yard in costume; say trick or treat to each one for a Halloween trinket, and help Mara find the five jack-o'-lanterns she hid around town. A prop plane drags a WELCOME TO WATERTOWN! banner across the sky every time you arrive, and five more portals home are tucked into the parks. Tess's Trinket Cart near the Square sells cottage decorations you can't get anywhere else, and more trinkets are hidden around town as purple glints.

**Pixel Palace Arcade:** the neon building in Willowbrook. Each cabinet plays one of our other games: Brick Ninja, Toilet Goalie, MegaMax, Slapanimal, Townies, Decay Envelope, Bell Weathered, Mind Arcade, Mise en Place, Crush Commando and MEGAzine BBS (which dials a real bulletin board). Through the bead curtain on the right is a dim back room with three curiosities: Big Apple Sam (an After Dark night skyline on an old CRT), Frisky (a music box on a pedestal) and Demoseed 40K (endless generated 90s demos). The Animal Sanctuary cabinet boots a real copy of this game inside itself: walk to the arcade in there and do it again, as deep as you dare. Every level down, the cabinet frame gets thicker, rounder and stranger, and a 🌀 counter shows how many games deep you are (games inside games never save). On the GitHub Pages site they open right inside the game (tap "Back to the arcade" to leave); elsewhere they open in a new tab.

**The Immortal Dungeon:** a cave has opened at the north end of Willowbrook, above Mabel's Market. Climb down and the game turns into a Doom-style 3D labyrinth (drawn with a raycaster, like the old Wolfenstein and Doom engines), with a minimap in the corner. Tap the minimap, or press M, for the whole map.

- **You start with just your fists.** Somewhere down there a chainsaw is waiting to be found. Hold A to rev it.
- **Everyday weapons** from Grizzle are cheaper and great against murder animals, though they can't touch the immortals: a Slingshot (150 coins), a Spiked Club that bonks two monsters and knocks them back (350), a Boomerang that flies out through everything and comes back to you (600), and a Frost Wand that freezes monsters solid for a few seconds (900).
- **Murder animals** roam the halls: Gnashrats, Skullbats, spitting Fangtoads, charging Razorboars and Howlers. They chase you through the maze, and they drop coins when you beat them.
- **Kind animals** don't need to be fought. Walk up and pet them: a Glowmoth heals you, a Lantern Snail lights up your map, and a Mossy Mole digs you up a gem.
- **The immortal beasts** live in four lairs in the corners of the labyrinth. Nothing ordinary can hurt them, and each one can only be stopped by its own ultra weapon from **Grizzle's Armory**. Grizzle stands by the cave in town and at the bottom of the ladder. Knock a beast out with the right weapon, then walk up and catch it before it wakes. Tamed, it shrinks to a cozy size and moves into your sanctuary.

| Beast | Where | Weapon that stops it | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dreadmaw, a cave crocodile | A lair | Thunder Harpoon | 1,800 |
| Bonestag, a skeleton stag with blue fire antlers | A lair | Moonsilver Bow (arrows pierce a whole row) | 3,000 |
| Hydrasaur, three heads of fire | A lair | Dragonbreath Blaster (hold A) | 4,500 |
| Night Mantis, which vanishes and reappears behind you | A lair | Starfire Cannon (exploding stars) | 7,000 |
| **Behemoth**, the enormous champion | **The Bone Colosseum**, a giant arena | Void Lance (a beam of pure night) | 12,000 |

- **The Bone Colosseum** is a giant round arena at the far end of the labyrinth. Inside, the Behemoth towers three times taller than the walls. It hurls boulders, stomps, charges, and calls in Gnashrats. It needs a Grand habitat once you've caught it.
- **Elevators** are tucked into dead ends all over the labyrinth, and they're the only way out. Each one goes somewhere strange up top: out of the plaza fountain, the back of an arcade cabinet, your own closet, the creature burrow, Pop and Gran's flowerbed, the Town Hall mail slot, a lobster trap at the end of the pier, a rabbit hole in Glimmer Meadow, a tombstone in Watertown, or the riverbank.
- **If you run out of hearts,** you don't die. You pass out and wake up somewhere odd in the main world, and a sneaky Gnashrat has run off with a fifth of your coins. Hearts slowly come back (up to three) if you stay out of trouble for a few seconds.
- The labyrinth has four regions (the Bone Halls, Mossy Deeps, Crystal Veins and Ember Vaults) plus treasure chests in the dead ends. It's the same maze every visit for your save, and your map stays filled in. When you play together, both players explore the same maze and share weapons and coins, but each fights their own monsters.
- **Controls down there:** ▲ ▼ walk, ◀ ▶ turn (Q and E sidestep on a keyboard), A attacks or uses whatever is in front of you, B swaps weapons, and the number keys pick a weapon.

**Saltwhistle Harbor:** a trail at the bottom right of Willowbrook, past Maple Lane, leads down to the sea. There's a pier, a lighthouse, Captain Marlow's shack, gulls, and the Leviathan, a great red ship tied up at the east pier. She isn't for sale yet: one day you'll be able to buy her and sail out after the most gargantuan creatures of the deep, for the Grand Aquarium (also coming one day; its lot is fenced off by the promenade).

**Seasons:** spring, summer, fall and winter each last 7 in-game days (about 4 minutes of play per day; napping in your cottage bed skips to the next morning). The whole world changes: blossoms in spring, golden leaves in fall, snow on the ground and roofs in winter, with matching weather and music. A new game starts in whatever season it really is outside. Each season brings two creatures you can only find then (Petalpip and Blossomfawn in spring, Sunnybee and Surfseal in summer, Pumpkit and Maplemunch in fall, Snowpuff and Twinkledeer in winter) and two seasonal quests in Willowbrook with seasonal decorations as rewards. In winter you can build snowmen in your sanctuary; they melt when spring comes. The season chip at the top shows the day.

**Giants:** Crystal Lake and Thunder Plains (unlocked through Professor Hoot's quests) are home to the Glimmerwhale, Mossback Titan and Skyhorn. Giants need a Grand habitat.

**Play together (online co-op):** tap **Together**. One player taps *Host my sanctuary* and gets a 4-letter code; the other types the code to join. Both explore, build, shop, take on quests and befriend creatures in the host's sanctuary (coins, creatures and habitats are shared; the host's device keeps the save). When both are in the same wild area they see the same creatures. Inside the Claude app this uses the artifact's live room; on GitHub Pages it connects peer to peer with PeerJS.

**The gate:** the game opens at the sanctuary gate. Tap it to swing the doors open (this also turns the music on), and the intro plays.

**Tutorial:** first-time players get a guided intro from Ranger Fern. Replay it any time from the ranger board.

**Play:** open `index.html`, or the GitHub Pages site for this repo.

**Controls:** arrows/WASD to move, Space = A (act), X = B (journal / close). On phones, use the on-screen D-pad and A/B buttons.
