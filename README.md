# Animal Sanctuary

A 16-bit style creature sanctuary game, dreamed up by Maya & Sam.

Explore magical wilds, befriend glowing creatures, bring them home to your sanctuary, and keep them happy so visitors pay to see them. Spend coins on new habitats and maps to new zones.

**Companions:** bring a creature along on an expedition and its ability works in the wilds — some lure creatures that only appear for them (a water song, a glow, a fire companion), others make catching easier, find more berries or treasure, or let you cross lava.

**Build mode:** tap Build to lay out your sanctuary Zoo Tycoon style: place habitats (meadow, pond, moon grove, ember rock), draw paths, and decorate with flowers, hedges, trees, ponds, benches, lamps, fountains and snack stands. Visitors walk only on paths and tip at habitats they pass, so every habitat needs a path beside it. Decorations add Charm, which raises tips.

**Willowbrook town:** walk out the bottom entrance of the sanctuary to reach town. Mabel's Market sells berries, golden apples and lucky clovers; Rosa's Bakery sells creature cookies; Bram's Builders sells grand habitats, new decorations, extra land and a cottage extension; Pip's Home Goods sells furniture, wallpaper and floors. Townsfolk with a yellow "!" have quests that unlock new areas and rewards. Trails at the edges of town lead to each wild area.

**Your cottage:** the purple-roofed house in town. Tap Decorate inside to place furniture, change wallpaper and floors, nap in your bed, and play the piano.

**Giants:** Crystal Lake and Thunder Plains (unlocked through Professor Hoot's quests) are home to the Glimmerwhale, Mossback Titan and Skyhorn. Giants need a Grand habitat.

**Play together (online co-op):** tap **Together**. One player taps *Host my sanctuary* and gets a 4-letter code; the other types the code to join. Both explore, build, shop, take on quests and befriend creatures in the host's sanctuary (coins, creatures and habitats are shared; the host's device keeps the save). When both are in the same wild area they see the same creatures. Inside the Claude app this uses the artifact's live room; on GitHub Pages it connects peer to peer with PeerJS.

**Tutorial:** first-time players get a guided intro from Ranger Fern. Replay it any time from the ranger board.

**Play:** open `index.html`, or the GitHub Pages site for this repo.

**Controls:** arrows/WASD to move, Space = A (act), X = B (journal / close). On phones, use the on-screen D-pad and A/B buttons.
